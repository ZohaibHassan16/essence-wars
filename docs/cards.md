# Starter Card Set Design Document

This document defines the complete starter set of 43 cards for the ESSENCE WARS engine. All cards follow the established balance formula: **Vanilla baseline = (Cost × 2) + 1 total stats**, with keywords and abilities adjusting this budget accordingly.

---

## Design Principles

1. **Curve Coverage** — Cards at every Essence cost from 1-8, concentrated in the 2-5 range
2. **Keyword Showcase** — Every keyword appears on at least 2 cards
3. **Archetype Seeds** — Cards supporting aggro, control, and midrange strategies
4. **Effect Variety** — Demonstrates different triggers, targets, and actions
5. **Interaction Depth** — Cards that create meaningful decisions
6. **Balance Adherence** — All cards follow stat/cost guidelines

## Key Interactions

These scenarios should be verified during engine testing:

1. **Piercing vs Guard** — Piercing damage only applies when the blocker dies
2. **Ranged bypasses Guard** — Ranged creatures can target non-Guard creatures
3. **Quick + Lethal** — Creature with both kills anything without taking damage
4. **Shield vs Lethal** — Shield absorbs hit, Lethal does not trigger (no damage dealt)
5. **Lifesteal + Piercing** — Lifesteal heals based on attack stat, not damage dealt
6. **Support stacking** — Multiple supports should stack their effects
7. **AoE effects** — Warlord Titan's entry should damage all enemies simultaneously
8. **Support-granted Rush** — Tactical Command enables immediate attacks for new creatures
