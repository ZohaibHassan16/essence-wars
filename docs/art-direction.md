# Essence Wars Art Direction Guide

This document establishes the visual identity for each faction in Essence Wars. All card art and commander portraits should follow these guidelines to ensure a cohesive, premium quality aesthetic across the game.

**Core Aesthetic**: 90s Magic: The Gathering card art style - painted fantasy illustration with dramatic lighting, heroic compositions, and classical oil painting techniques.

**Reference Artists**: Frank Frazetta (dynamic poses, heroic figures), Greg Rutkowski (detailed fantasy environments), classical MTG artists (Rebecca Guay, Terese Nielsen, Ron Spencer).

---

## Argentum Combine (Order & Industry)

### Faction Identity
The Argentum Combine represents civilization, order, and technological progress. They are defenders, engineers, and artificers who believe in the power of organization and precision machinery.

### Color Palette
| Element | Colors |
|---------|--------|
| **Primary** | White marble, polished brass, white steel |
| **Accent** | Gold filigree, soft blue essence energy |
| **Energy/Magic** | Glowing blue essence (NEVER orange or fire) |
| **Lighting** | Golden sunlight reflecting off brass, blue sky always visible |

### Materials & Architecture
- **Foundation**: Art Deco geometric patterns (symmetrical, ordered, angular filigree)
- **Materials**: Polished brass (mirror-finish), white marble (pristine), white steel
- **Features**:
  - White marble columns, arches, and classical temples
  - Brass reinforcement plates with geometric strengthening runes
  - Steam-powered machinery and brass conduits
  - Blue essence crystal arrays
  - Defensive fortifications (ramparts, gates, towers)

### Character Archetypes

**Soldiers & Defenders**
- Polished white and gold armor with Art Deco geometric patterns
- Brass accents and reinforcement plating
- Large shields bearing the Argentum sun-gear insignia
- Confident, determined, disciplined demeanor
- Athletic builds, heroic/noble poses

**Engineers & Artificers**
- White coats/robes with brass mechanical augmentations
- Brass goggles with blue lenses
- Surrounded by tools, blueprints, precision equipment
- Intelligent, capable expressions

**Constructs & Automata**
- Brass humanoid frames with Art Deco patterns
- Blue glowing essence cores visible through chest windows
- Blue sensor eyes and joints
- Steam venting from pressure valves

### Mood & Tone
- Professional and organized
- Noble and dignified
- Optimistic and bright (blue skies, golden sunlight)
- Industrial but beautiful

### LoRA Recommendations
- Characters: `frazetta:0.4, classical-painting:0.6`
- Constructs: `rutkowski:0.4, classical-painting:0.7`

### Always Avoid
- Orange, red, or fire (enemy faction colors)
- Gothic or dark architecture
- Overly organic or soft imagery
- Furnace/molten aesthetics
- Dark, moody lighting

## Symbiote Circles (Growth & Adaptation)

### Faction Identity
The Symbiote Circles represent primal nature, the hunt, and evolutionary adaptation. They are druids, beast masters, and pack warriors who draw power from the ancient forests and bond with nature's predators.

**Key Shift**: This faction should evoke **Urza's Saga-era MTG nature magic** - primordial forests, great beasts, ancient druids - NOT alien biopunk or sci-fi insectoid aesthetics.

### Color Palette
| Element | Colors |
|---------|--------|
| **Primary** | Deep forest green, rich earth brown, moss tones |
| **Accent** | Purple and green bioluminescence (magical, not alien) |
| **Energy/Magic** | Green druidic energy, purple spirit magic |
| **Lighting** | Dappled forest light, moonlit clearings, bioluminescent fungi |

### Materials & Environment
- **Foundation**: Ancient primordial forests, sacred groves, jungle temples
- **Materials**: Leather, fur, bone, living wood, natural armor
- **Features**:
  - Massive ancient trees with glowing fungi
  - Sacred groves with bioluminescent plants
  - Jungle clearings with stone ruins
  - Pack dens and hunting grounds
  - Druid circles with standing stones

### Character Archetypes

**Female Characters**
- Elegant druid queens and shamans
- Using beauty and intelligence as assets of power
- Natural materials: leather, fur, living vines, flower crowns
- Athletic builds with confident, commanding poses
- Connection to nature visible (animal companions, plant magic)

**Male Characters**
- Muscular hunters and pack alphas
- Powerful stances showing physical prowess
- Feral energy but noble bearing
- Tribal markings (bioluminescent paint, not mutations)
- Often depicted with predator companions

**Beasts & Creatures**
- **Wolves, dire wolves, wolf packs** - for swarm/rush creatures
- **Great cats** (tigers, panthers, lions) - for lethal/stalker creatures
- **Serpents and wurms** - for large powerful creatures
- **Bears and boars** - for defensive/guard creatures
- **Forest spirits and treants** - for regenerating/structure creatures
- **Birds of prey** - for ranged/scout creatures

### Creature Design Philosophy

**FROM (Remove)**:
- Chitinous armor, compound eyes, mandibles
- Alien insectoid aesthetics
- Bio-engineered, synthetic feel
- "Hive mind" terminology
- Xenomorph/Zerg visual language

**TO (Embrace)**:
- Fur, scales, feathers, natural armor
- Fantasy beast aesthetics (wolves, cats, serpents)
- Evolved, organic, primordial feel
- "Pack bond" terminology
- Urza's Saga nature magic visual language

### Volatile/Decay Creatures
- Fungal/decay themed (rotting plants, toxic spores, corrupted spirits)
- NOT bio-engineered explosives
- Sickly plants, poison mushrooms, plague-touched beasts
- Death and decay as natural cycle, not alien biology

### Mood & Tone
- Primal and wild
- Majestic and dangerous
- Ancient and mystical
- Predator energy - confident, not mindless

### LoRA Recommendations
- Human characters: `frazetta:0.5, classical-painting:0.5`
- Beasts and environments: `rutkowski:0.4, classical-painting:0.6`
- Decay/volatile: `rutkowski:0.5, classical-painting:0.5`

### Always Avoid
- Alien/insectoid aesthetics (compound eyes, mandibles, chitin plates)
- Sci-fi bio-engineering language
- "Hive" terminology (use "pack", "grove", "circle" instead)
- Xenomorph/Zerg visual references
- Mechanical or industrial elements

### Terminology Replacement Guide

| Old Term | New Term |
|----------|----------|
| Hive | Pack / Grove / Circle |
| Swarm | Pack / Flock / Pride |
| Chitin | Fur / Scales / Natural armor |
| Broodling | Pack whelp / Young predator |
| Hiveling | Pack runner / Forest sprite |
| Compound eyes | Predator eyes / Golden eyes |
| Mandibles | Fangs / Jaws / Tusks |
| Carapace | Hide / Pelt / Scales |
| Bio-engineered | Evolved / Blessed by nature |
| Hive mind | Pack bond / Spirit link |

## Obsidion Syndicate (Knowledge & Ambition)

### Faction Identity
The Obsidion Syndicate represents forbidden knowledge, dark ambition, and the pursuit of power at any cost. They are vampire lords, shadow assassins, void cultists, and time mages who have traded morality for efficacy. They sustain themselves through life-draining magic while striking from shadows.

**Core Theme**: Gothic Victorian elegance meets vampiric horror - aristocratic predators in dark cathedrals.

### Color Palette
| Element | Colors |
|---------|--------|
| **Primary** | Crimson blood red, deep black, pale aristocratic skin |
| **Accent** | Neon blue essence (magical energy), gold trim on nobles |
| **Energy/Magic** | Crimson blood tendrils, neon blue essence tubes/vials |
| **Lighting** | Candlelit chambers, moonlight through stained glass, shadow-filled |

### Materials & Architecture
- **Foundation**: Gothic Victorian architecture (pointed arches, flying buttresses, spires)
- **Materials**: Black stone, dark iron, velvet, leather, glass essence vials
- **Features**:
  - Gothic cathedrals with stained glass windows
  - Victorian manor houses and dark libraries
  - Assassin guild hideouts with shadow-filled corners
  - Blood altars and ritual chambers
  - Spire towers reaching into storm clouds

### Character Archetypes

**Vampire Aristocrats**
- Elegant pale figures in crimson velvet and black silk
- Sharp aristocratic features (high cheekbones, piercing eyes)
- Gold jewelry and ornate accessories
- Blood magic swirling around their hands
- Regal, predatory bearing

**Shadow Assassins**
- Sleek black leather armor revealing athletic forms
- Concealed daggers with venom-coated blades
- Sharp elven-like features, calculating eyes
- Shadows clinging to their forms
- Confident, deadly poses

**Void Cultists**
- Dark robes with crimson trim and arcane symbols
- Neon blue essence tubes attached to gauntlets/equipment
- Pale, intense expressions of forbidden knowledge
- Channeling dark magic from ancient tomes
- Surrounded by candles and ritual circles

**Spirits & Shades**
- Translucent, ethereal forms of neon blue energy
- Flowing robes dissolving into shadow/mist
- Haunting beauty with otherworldly eyes
- Often ephemeral, fading at the edges
- Ghostly trail effects

### Keyword Visual Themes

| Keyword | Visual Representation |
|---------|----------------------|
| **Lifesteal** | Crimson blood tendrils flowing from victim to attacker |
| **Quick** | Blur effects, temporal distortion, clockwork motifs |
| **Stealth** | Shadows clinging, partial invisibility, dark corners |
| **Ephemeral** | Translucent forms, fading edges, ghostly glow |
| **Lethal** | Venom dripping, skull motifs, death imagery |

### Mood & Tone
- Dark elegance and sophistication
- Predatory grace
- Forbidden knowledge and ancient secrets
- Seductive danger
- Gothic romance undertones

### LoRA Recommendations
- Vampires/Nobles: `classical-painting:0.5, frazetta:0.4`
- Assassins: `frazetta:0.5, classical-painting:0.5`
- Spirits/Shades: `rutkowski:0.4, classical-painting:0.6`
- Environments: `rutkowski:0.5, classical-painting:0.5`

### Always Avoid
- Bright, cheerful colors (no yellows, bright greens)
- Natural/organic settings (forests, jungles)
- Industrial/mechanical elements (that's Argentum)
- Crude or brutish characters (Obsidion is elegant)
- Modern clothing or technology

## Free-Walkers (Neutral)

### Faction Identity
The Free-Walkers are mercenaries, sellswords, and wanderers who pledge allegiance to no faction. They work for coin, not ideology. Their equipment is practical and often salvaged from all three factions, creating a distinctive hybrid aesthetic.

**Core Theme**: Wasteland mercenaries with mixed-faction gear - pragmatic survivors in a frontier world.

### Color Palette
| Element | Colors |
|---------|--------|
| **Primary** | Dusty browns, earth tones, sun-bleached leather |
| **Accent** | Copper/brass metallics, weathered steel |
| **Equipment** | Mixed faction elements (brass + dark leather + bone) |
| **Lighting** | Desert sun, golden hour, campfire warmth |

### Materials & Environment
- **Foundation**: Desert wastelands, frontier towns, trade roads
- **Materials**: Worn leather, salvaged metal, practical cloth, rope
- **Features**:
  - Desert ruins and abandoned outposts
  - Mercenary camps with tents and supply crates
  - Trade caravans on dusty roads
  - Frontier taverns and guild halls
  - Battlefield aftermath scenes

### Character Archetypes

**Mercenary Warriors**
- Practical armor mixing elements from all factions
- Weathered but well-maintained equipment
- Battle scars and veteran bearing
- Confident, professional demeanor
- Coin purses and contract scrolls visible

**Scouts & Rangers**
- Light leather armor for mobility
- **Fantasy crossbows or composite bows** (NOT modern firearms)
- Keen, alert expressions
- Desert camouflage and practical gear
- Often with animal companions (hawks, dogs)

**Traveling Merchants**
- Colorful but dusty trade clothes
- Laden with goods and wares
- Shrewd, calculating expressions
- Pack animals and carts nearby
- Gold coins and scales visible

**Support Personnel**
- Healers with herb pouches and bandages
- Engineers with tools and contraptions
- Mystics with protective wards
- Practical, utilitarian appearance

### Equipment Design Philosophy

**The "Hybrid Gear" Aesthetic**
Neutral characters should visibly wear equipment salvaged/purchased from multiple factions:

| Faction Origin | Visual Element |
|----------------|----------------|
| **Argentum** | Brass pauldrons, gear insignia, blue-tinted goggles |
| **Symbiote** | Bone trinkets, leather straps, fur trim |
| **Obsidion** | Dark leather pieces, crimson cloth accents |

### Weapons (IMPORTANT)

**USE**: Fantasy crossbows, composite bows, throwing knives, practical swords, spears, shields
**AVOID**: Modern firearms, rifles, pistols, guns of any kind

The Neutral faction should feel like classic fantasy mercenaries, not post-apocalyptic soldiers.

### Mood & Tone
- Practical pragmatism
- Battle-worn competence
- Loyalty to coin, not ideology
- Rugged survival
- Professional neutrality

### LoRA Recommendations
- Warriors: `frazetta:0.5, classical-painting:0.5`
- Scouts/Rangers: `frazetta:0.4, classical-painting:0.6`
- Merchants/Support: `rutkowski:0.4, classical-painting:0.6`
- Environments: `rutkowski:0.5, classical-painting:0.5`

### Always Avoid
- Modern weapons (guns, rifles, firearms)
- Strong faction alignment (too much of one faction's aesthetic)
- Clean, pristine equipment (should look used)
- Gothic or heavily industrial settings
- Bright faction-specific colors dominating

## Technical Specifications

### Image Dimensions
- **Card Art**: 704 x 896 pixels (portrait orientation)
- **Commander Portraits**: Same dimensions, stored in `/static/portrait/`

### Generation Settings (FLUX Dev)
```bash
--cfg-scale 1.0 --sampling-method euler --steps 20
```

### File Formats
- Source: PNG (from generation)
- Final: WebP (quality 95, for game use)

### File Locations
- Card art: `/crates/essence-wars-ui/static/cards/core_set/{id}.webp`
- Commander portraits: `/crates/essence-wars-ui/static/portrait/{name_snake_case}.webp`
- Art prompts: `/data/art/prompts/core_set/{faction}.yaml`

---

## Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any card art, verify:

- [ ] Matches faction color palette
- [ ] No text or watermarks visible
- [ ] Correct aspect ratio (portrait)
- [ ] Painterly illustration style (not photorealistic)
- [ ] Clear subject with good composition
- [ ] Appropriate mood for card type
- [ ] No forbidden visual elements for faction
