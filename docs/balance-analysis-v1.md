---

## ROOT CAUSES

1. **Ephemeral is a HUGE downside**, not a bonus - creature dies at end of turn before dealing damage again
2. **Lethal at -2 cost is too expensive** - 1/1 and 2/2 Lethal creatures die to everything
3. **Quick + Stealth combo (-3)** makes Silent Assassin unplayable at 4/2
4. **Argentum Guard creatures have excessive health** - 6 HP at 3 cost, 8 HP at 5 cost
5. **Obsidion has no board presence** - creatures die before Lifesteal generates value
6. **The Eternal One at 8 mana** is too slow and understatted for its cost

## PROPOSED BALANCE CHANGES

### NERF ARGENTUM (reduce defensive power)

| Card | Current | Proposed | Reason |
|------|---------|----------|--------|
| Cogwork Defender | 2/6 Guard | **2/5** Guard | -1 HP, still strong |
| Iron Colossus | 3/8 Guard | **3/7** Guard | -1 HP, still a wall |
| Clockwork Medic | 1/4, heal 3 | 1/4, **heal 2** | Reduce sustain |
| Grand Constructor | 5/8 Guard, +2/+3 | **4/6** Guard, **+1/+2** | Major nerf to legendary |

### BUFF SYMBIOTE (make Lethal viable)

| Card | Current | Proposed | Reason |
|------|---------|----------|--------|
| Venom Fang | 2/2 Lethal | **2/3** Lethal | Survives 2-attack creatures |
| Parasitic Larva | 1/1 Lethal | **1/2** Lethal | Doesn't die to everything |
| Pack Hunter | 3/1 Rush | **3/2** Rush | Can attack twice |
| Alpha Predator | 5/4 Rush+Lethal | **5/5** Rush+Lethal | Rare should be strong |
| Hive Guardian | 3/5 Guard+Regen | **3/6** Guard+Regen | Faction's only Guard |
| Acid Spitter | 3/2 Ranged | **3/3** Ranged | Matches budget |

| Card | Current | Proposed | Reason |
