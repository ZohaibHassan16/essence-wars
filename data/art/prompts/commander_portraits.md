## Standard Settings for All Portraits

- **Model:** flux-dev-q8.gguf
- **Dimensions:** 704x896 (portrait format)
- **CFG Scale:** 1.0
- **Sampling:** Euler method
- **Steps:** 20
- **Composition:** Three-quarter view waist-up shot
- **Framing:** Head to waist, showing upper body and command presence
- **Focus:** Sharp detail on face with secondary focus on torso/equipment
- **Background:** Softly blurred, contextual

### Art Style Consistency

- **Base:** 90s Magic the Gathering card art
- **Technique:** Painted fantasy illustration with visible brushstrokes
- **Lighting:** Dramatic, character-defining
- **Texture:** Oil painting quality
- **Color:** Faction-specific palettes as defined in style guide

### Portrait-Specific Guidelines

1. Face remains the primary focal point - well-lit and clearly visible
2. Eyes are critical - capture personality and power
3. Show torso, armor, and equipment to display commander aesthetic
4. Hands should be visible with purposeful gestures (commanding, wielding, summoning)
5. Body language conveys authority, power, and faction identity
6. Framing cuts off at waist - no legs visible
7. Background elements support character but don't distract
8. Lighting should sculpt both facial features and body dramatically
9. Pose should be dynamic but not full action - commanding presence
10. Equipment/weapons should be contextual to their abilities
